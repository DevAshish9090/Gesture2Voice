# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates in this project.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, consent, and intellectual-property rights.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sharing private information without permission, and other disruptive behaviour are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the project maintainer. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled with appropriate action, which may include removing content or restricting participation.
