Capabilities

Everything you need
to communicate.

Built for accessibility from the ground up — fast, reliable, and designed for the people who need it most.

01
Real-time Detection

Webcam-powered gesture recognition with a 4-frame stability filter — only confirms a word when your hand is steady and intentional.

≤ 80ms latency
02
Sentence Building

Words accumulate naturally into full sentences. A 30-second inactivity timer auto-resets, keeping the flow clean and uninterrupted.

30s auto-reset
03
Text to Speech

Your built sentence is spoken aloud instantly. Choose from four accent voices — American, British, Indian, or Australian English.

4 accent voices
04
Emergency Mode

A dedicated emergency button continuously repeats "I need help immediately" aloud until manually dismissed — no gestures or typing required.

Button → repeat → dismiss
05
Confidence Display

A live confidence score and progress bar shows how certain the model is about each detection, so you always know when a gesture was read correctly.

Live score overlay
06
Sentence History

Your last 5 spoken sentences are saved and replayable with one click — useful in fast-moving conversations or care settings.

Last 5 sentences
